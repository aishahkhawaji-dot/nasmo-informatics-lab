01 • Before the algorithms

First, learn how to think.

Informatics is not about memorizing code. It is about understanding a problem, recognizing its structure, and choosing a precise way to solve it.

Your Problem-Solving Toolbox

Before you move into the stages, learn the thinking process you will use throughout Informatics.

02 · OBSERVEWhat pattern or structure do I see?
03 · CHOOSEWhich idea fits the constraints?
04 · CODECan I express the idea clearly?
05 · CHECKWhat happens on edge cases?
